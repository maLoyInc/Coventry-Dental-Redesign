All-on-4 replaces a full upper or lower arch with a fixed bridge carried on four
implants. It is the route for people who have lost most of their teeth, or who
are facing that, and do not want a removable denture.

Because the implants are angled to use the bone you already have, most people
avoid a separate bone graft. A temporary fixed bridge is usually fitted the same
day the implants are placed, so you do not leave without teeth.

The final bridge is fitted once healing is complete, typically three to six
months later.
