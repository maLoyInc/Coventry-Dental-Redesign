Invisalign straightens teeth with a series of clear, removable aligners. Each
one moves the teeth a little further than the last, and you change to the next
set roughly every one to two weeks.

They come out for eating and cleaning, which is the practical difference from a
fixed brace: nothing is off the menu and your normal brushing routine does not
change.

Most adult cases run six to eighteen months depending on how far the teeth have
to move. A retainer afterwards is what keeps the result.
