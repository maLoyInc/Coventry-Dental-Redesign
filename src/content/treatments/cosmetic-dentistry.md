Cosmetic work covers veneers, composite bonding, crowns and whitening. On their
own each is a small procedure; the difference is planning them as one result
rather than one tooth at a time.

Treatment starts with a discussion about what you actually want changed, followed
by a plan you see before anything is prepared. Where a change is irreversible —
veneers in particular — that is said plainly at the planning stage, not after.

Simple bonding or whitening can be done in one or two visits. A full plan is
staged over several weeks.
