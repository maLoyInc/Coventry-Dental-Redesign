A single dental implant replaces one missing tooth from the root up. A titanium
post is placed into the jawbone, left to fuse over a healing period, and then
restored with a crown matched to the teeth either side of it.

Unlike a bridge, an implant does not rely on the neighbouring teeth for support,
so healthy teeth are left untouched. Unlike a denture, it is fixed — it does not
move when you eat or speak.

Treatment usually runs over three to six months, most of which is healing time
rather than appointments. You are not left with a gap while that happens.
